# Privacy Policy for KeepGo

**Last updated: October 2026**

KeepGo ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app operates and handles user information.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
KeepGo is built as a pure, offline-first application:
- We DO NOT collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information.
- We DO NOT use third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs.
- No account registration or login is required.

### 2. Local Storage & iCloud Sync
- All your focus records, statistics, and settings are stored locally on your device using native sandboxed storage.
- If you enable iCloud sync, your focus data is transmitted and stored securely in your private Apple iCloud account via Apple's official `NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore` API. We have no access to your iCloud data.

### 3. In-App Purchases
All purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store via StoreKit 2. We do not process or store any financial or credit card information.

# KeepGo 隐私政策

**最后更新日期：2026 年 10 月**

KeepGo（以下简称“我们”）高度重视用户的隐私保护。本隐私政策旨在向您说明本应用如何处理用户数据。

### 1. 零数据收集原则
KeepGo 是一款纯粹的离线优先独立应用：
- 我们不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人身份信息。
- 我们绝不嵌入任何第三方广告、追踪或商业化统计SDK。

所有 PRO 特权购买均通过 Apple 官方 App Store 进行安全结算，我们不会接触、亦不保存您的任何支付或银行卡信息。
